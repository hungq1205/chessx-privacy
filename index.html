This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game ChessX: Morph Chess Puzzle (the "Game", "we", "us") for Android handles information about you and your device. It applies only to the Game. By playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in and no analytics SDK.
  • We, the developer, do not directly collect, store or sell any personal information.
  • The Game shows ads. Our advertising partners collect device information, including the Android Advertising ID, to serve and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

2. Information we collect directly

We do not collect any personal information directly. Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, this local data is deleted.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you.

3. Advertising

The Game displays ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity's ad mediation platform), which selects ads from the ad networks listed below. These third parties may automatically collect and process the following data from your device:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and other device identifiers
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language, screen size and network/carrier type
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or clicked, and app usage events related to ads
  • Diagnostic and performance data related to ad delivery

This data is used by these partners to serve ads, personalize ads (where allowed), limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance, attribute installs, and detect and prevent fraud. The data is collected and processed by the ad partners under their own privacy policies:

4. In-app purchases

Purchases inside the Game are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so that we can deliver the item. We do not receive or store your card number, billing address or other payment details.

5. How to control ads and the Advertising ID

You can limit how your Advertising ID is used at any time in your Android settings:

  • Android 12 and later: open Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices Settings > Google > Ads) and tap Delete advertising ID. Apps will then receive a blank ID instead of your real one.
  • Older Android versions: open Settings > Google > Ads and turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization, or tap Reset advertising ID to get a new one.

You will still see ads after opting out, but they will be less tailored to you. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK or certain US states), you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. The only parties that receive data from the Game are:

  • Advertising partners listed in section 3, for ad serving, measurement, attribution and fraud prevention.
  • Google, as the provider of Google Play and Google Play Billing.
  • Authorities, if we are required to disclose information by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the Game. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own privacy policies; you can contact them directly or reset your Advertising ID (section 5) to break the link to previously collected data.

8. Security

Data sent between the Game and our advertising partners is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we choose reputable partners and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled through your device's ad settings (section 5) or directly with the advertising partners in section 3.
